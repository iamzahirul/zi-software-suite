টেইলার্স, বুটিক ও লেডিস টেইলারিংয়ের জন্য: কাস্টমারের প্রতিটি পোশাকের মাপ সেভ, মাপ-ডিজাইনসহ অর্ডার, ট্রায়াল ও ডেলিভারির তারিখ, মাস্টারের জন্য কাটিং স্লিপ, কারিগরের পিস-মজুরি, অগ্রিম ও বাকি, আর ঈদের চাপে কোন দিন ফুল তা আগেই দেখা।

## Features
- **Measurements:** shirt, panjabi, pant, pajama, kameez, salwar, blouse, coat, borka and more — each with the measurements Bangladeshi tailors take (লম্বা, বুক, কোমর, হিপ, পুট, হাতা, মুহুরী, গলা, রান…), ½-inch values, note. Saved on the customer and filled in automatically on the next order.
- **Order:** mobile lookup for old customers, several garments per order, style chips (collar, pocket, cuff, slit, piping, lining, slim / loose fit…), making charge, fabric price, karigor, urgent flag, trial and delivery dates, discount, advance (cash / bKash / Nagad / card).
- **Prints:** customer memo (A5 or 80 mm) with barcode, terms and a tear-off **fabric tag**; **cutting slip** for the master with every measurement in big numbers and the style choices.
- **Stages:** received → cutting → sewing → ready → delivered; each piece marked sewn by its karigor; one-tap "ready" SMS/WhatsApp; delivery with final payment (or leave a due).
- **Eid rush planner:** pieces due on each of the next 14 days against the shop's daily capacity — busy / full days in colour; warning while booking a full day.
- **Dashboard:** deliver today, late, ready but not collected, trials today, cash today, total due; search by order no., name or mobile.
- **Karigor wages:** wage per piece by garment, pieces in hand / done, wage earned, paid, balance; weekly payments; wage statement with signatures.
- **Rates:** making charge and karigor wage per garment, shop margin.
- **Reports:** day by day, by garment, payment methods, customer dues; print / CSV.
- Bangla/English, dark mode, PIN lock, backup, offline; optional cloud sync (Supabase) for several branches.
